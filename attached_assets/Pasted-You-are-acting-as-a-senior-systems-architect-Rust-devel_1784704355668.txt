    “Miles IT Support is requesting remote access to help resolve your issue.”

    - Administrator name
    - Department
    - Reason
    - Ticket number
    - Requested capabilities
13. Employee can:
    - Accept
    - Decline
    - Accept view-only
14. On acceptance, a short-lived session is created.
15. Administrator opens the remote viewer.
16. Employee sees a persistent active-session banner.
17. Either party can end the session.
18. Session access expires immediately when ended.
19. Session summary is written to the audit log.

If the device is offline:

- Disable connection.
- Show last seen.
- Allow “Notify me when device is online”.
- Do not create a permanent connection credential.
